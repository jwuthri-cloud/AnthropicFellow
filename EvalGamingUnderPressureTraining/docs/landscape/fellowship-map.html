What changes from the $500 version

  • Organisms trained with RL, so behaviors emerge from training instead of being implanted.
  • 32B to 70B models, where released tools exist: natural language autoencoders, introspection adapters and Gemma Scope 2.
  • Several seeds and ablations per result, which reviewers expect.
  • A benchmark or toolkit as a deliverable, not only a single finding.
  • A mentor who has published on the topic, which makes speed and framing much easier.
02 · Candidate projects

Fellowship-scale projects, novelty-checked

Checked on 11 Oct 2026 against arXiv, OpenReview, ICML 2026 workshops, lab blogs, LessWrong, and MATS and SPAR project lists. The mentor-fit column lists Anthropic researchers who have published on the topic. It does not say who is mentoring your cohort.

Recommendation

Pitch eval gaming under training pressure first. Keep the sandbagging benchmark as the safer second choice.

The eval-gaming project spends the fellowship's compute where it counts: real RL runs against a monitor, which nobody has published. It has two arms, so if one stalls the other still yields a paper. The sandbagging benchmark costs less and carries less risk. Both sit inside Anthropic's auditing agenda, and the same researchers have published on both, which helps when matching with a mentor.

05 · How this was built

Method and caveats

Method

  • The landscape, timeline and state-of-the-art cards reuse the verified research behind the $500 version: a 106-agent deep-research pass plus four follow-up agents.
  • Two more agents ran adversarial novelty checks on ten fellowship-scale ideas on 11 Oct 2026, with several search phrasings per idea.
  • Program facts come from the 2026 Fellows program page and the job posting, both read on 11 Oct 2026.

Caveats

  • Mentor names are researchers who published on a topic. They are not confirmed mentors for any cohort.
  • Compute figures are planning estimates at $2 to $2.50 per H100-hour, not quotes.
  • Several closest papers are preprints from the last few weeks, read at abstract level. Fast-moving areas will look different by your start date, so re-run the novelty search before you pitch.
  • The public pages do not say whether fellows can bring their own idea or how mentors are matched.
