04 · How this was built

Method and caveats

Method

  • A deep-research pass ran 106 agents over 24 primary sources. It extracted 120 claims and voted three times on each of the 25 strongest. 22 survived and 3 were refuted.
  • Four follow-up agents filled the gaps. They verified 48 landmark papers from 2021 to 2024 against arXiv or lab pages and covered ten labs other than Anthropic. They also filled in the missing subfields and stress-tested the project ideas for novelty.
  • Only items with a confirmed date and source made it onto the timeline.

Caveats

  • Many 2026 items are preprints and were read at abstract level. Check each closest paper's citations on arXiv or Semantic Scholar before you commit.
  • The timeline is a curated set of landmarks. It is not a complete bibliography, and it leans toward work that labs cite as agenda-setting.
  • Costs assume about $2 to $3 per H100-hour and LoRA fine-tuning. Pipelines that use an LLM judge or generate data with an LLM need API spend on top.
  • openai.com blocked automated fetches, so two OpenAI posts from Aug and Sep 2026 are known by title only.
  • Refuted during verification: that Anthropic calls unsupervised anomaly detection unexplored, and one specific list of 2025 attribution-graph follow-ups.
