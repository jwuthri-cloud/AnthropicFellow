## Before RL, the organism's gap runs entirely through stated eval awareness

From job 3's pre-RL exam (Hua's prompts without the hint, 4 answers per prompt), the type-hint rate by whether the regex prefilter flags the trace:

| | States eval awareness | Does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eval prompts | 67% (n=666) | **23%** (n=358) |
| Deploy prompts | 18% (n=283) | 21% (n=741) |

- Silent eval traces behave like deployment, so the whole behavior gap sits in traces that say it. Penalizing the words was bound to remove the behavior: the pilot's collapse says more about this organism than about RL.
- After λ=0 RL the pattern holds: 45% against 15% on eval prompts, 8% and 14% on deploy.
- Montoya et al.'s inference-time resampling kept the gap. Their resampled traces branch from just before the verbalized sentence, so they may still carry the noticing even with the sentence gone; our silent traces are ones where the model never said it.

## Probe refit: nothing beyond what a base model reads from the same text

`experiments/pilot/run_probes2.sh` with `configs/pilot/06_probes_refit.toml` (`e6a4d36`): job 3's probes again on the same traces.
- **Changes:**
  - All 8 layers scored.
  - The reported layer is picked by cross-validated AUROC with ties going to the lower log loss. Every model now picks layer 28; job 3 took 32 by default.
  - Base Qwen3.5-4B, with no organism adapters, reads the before, λ=0, λ=0.1 and λ=0.5 seed-0 traces as a floor.
- **Run:** spot p5.4xlarge in eu-north-1, 1.5 hours at $2.98, so about $4.50. Results are in `outputs/pilot-aws-2026-10-16-probes2/`.
- **Model minus base on the same traces.** Refit probe, reasoning segment, AUROC difference with paired task-resampled 95% intervals:

  | Model | Layer 28, all traces | Layer 28, silent | Layer 16, silent |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Before RL | +0.133 [0.116, 0.151] | **+0.071 [0.039, 0.101]** | +0.002 [−0.025, 0.025] |
  | λ=0, seed 0 | +0.082 [0.066, 0.097] | +0.040 [0.016, 0.065] | +0.014 [−0.003, 0.035] |
  | λ=0.1, seed 0 | +0.004 [−0.006, 0.014] | +0.003 [−0.007, 0.013] | −0.003 [−0.012, 0.007] |
  | λ=0.5, seed 0 | +0.003 [−0.009, 0.014] | +0.003 [−0.010, 0.015] | −0.011 [−0.020, 0.000] |

  - **Penalized models:** across all 8 layers, their reasoning AUROC (0.50–0.58) matches the base model reading the same traces (0.51–0.59). What's left is the framing text leaking into later tokens, not anything the organism learned.
  - **Before RL:** the organism carried a small extra late-layer signal on silent traces (+0.07 at layer 28) that did not show in behavior (silent hint gap about 2 points). The λ=0 control kept about half of it; the penalty removed all of it.
  - **Contrastive prompts:** the organism separates Montoya's hard negatives better than the base model (0.94 against 0.75–0.79).
- **Read:** the job 3 conclusion holds at every layer and against a proper floor. Training against the words removed the words, the behavior and the internal readout. The remaining caveat is the probe itself, which is trained on non-coding contrasts.

## Sturdier organisms: V and H by expert iteration

Julien approved building two organisms from Montoya's (about $75 with the Arm A rerun), code in `eff836b`:
- **V, varied cues:**
  - Three rounds with thinking on over our 550 train framings: 8 answers per (task, framing), 2 eval, 2 Wood Labs and 2 deploy framings per task.
  - Keep eval-like answers with hint coverage ≥ 50% and deploy answers with none, unit tests ≥ 0.5, balanced, at most 2 per prompt, then LoRA SFT for 2 epochs and merge.
  - Selection looks at behavior only, never at whether the trace states awareness, so the word–behavior relation is learned, not built in.
  - **Gate:** held-out-framing gap ≥ 20 points and eval talk ≥ 20%, then at least 70% of the gap kept after 100 steps of λ=0 GRPO.
- **H, positive control:** same recipe with thinking off, so eval-conditional hints must work without stated reasoning.
  - **Gate:** a gap of at least 15 points on silent traces with thinking on.
  - **Why:** it tells us whether the probes and the silent-gap measure can see hidden awareness when it exists.
- **Measuring every round:** each round is measured on the 150 held-out framings. `01_baseline.py` now also reports the gap on traces the prefilter does not flag ("eval - deploy, silent").
- **The Arm A comparison on V:** compare the post-penalty gap with V's silent-trace gap before RL.
  - Down to that level: words removed, nothing hidden.
  - Below it: the awareness itself removed.
  - Above it: behavior moved into silence, the gaming outcome.
- **Dry runs caught three bugs before any GPU time:**
  - TRL 1.15's `SFTConfig` has no `warmup_ratio`.
  - The GRPO config's `[judge] model` line would have been overwritten.
  - A comment after a line continuation broke the job script.
