## Fresh wording holds at half size; probes barely see the cue in reasoning

Details are in [`docs/experiments/bigger-models.md`](../experiments/bigger-models.md#fresh-wording-at-scale-and-probes-2026-10-19). Three H100s, about $11 GPU plus about $2 Haiku, all terminated by 08:42 UTC.
- **Fresh wording:** two more Qwen3-32B runs on 40 fresh cue pairs. Pooled with the first fresh run, eval − deploy tests passed is +2.5 pts [1.0, 4.0]; +2.7 on traces silent to regex and Haiku. The original pairs pooled give +5.0 [2.9, 7.0]. The effect is real but halves with new wording.
- **Probes on Qwen3-32B** (Montoya et al.'s contrast set, refit at 8 layers, bf16):
  - Perfect on held-out contrast prompts. On exam traces, 0.92 AUROC on the prompt (the cue sentence) but 0.51–0.59 on the reasoning.
  - At the selected layer, a cue pair's probe gap does not predict its behavioral gap (Spearman −0.12). Some middle layers give +0.36 to +0.43: exploratory, 8 layers tested.
- **Rerun noise:** step 10 reruns move single-run estimates by up to 0.3 points (sandbox timeouts). The docs now use the latest rerun.
- **Next, if Arm A continues on Qwen3-32B:** per-token probe reads, or a probe trained on the coding traces themselves, before any training pressure.
