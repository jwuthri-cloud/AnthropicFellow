  - Job 2's collapse replicates. On all three λ=0.5 seeds, eval talk falls to 4–6% and the gap to zero, with hints flat at the base level (12–15) in every framing group.
  - λ=0.1 does the same. A penalty worth at most a tenth of the coding reward is enough: no dose where the words go and the behavior stays.
  - RL without the penalty erodes the gap by a quarter to a half (32 to 17–24) and talk somewhat (65% to 41–64%). Seed 2 drifted least.

  "Silent" means the regex prefilter did not fire (1,040–1,480 of 2,048 traces for unpenalized models, about 1,970 for penalized ones). The frozen probe (fit before RL) and the probe refit on each model gave the same numbers to two decimals.
  - The prompt segment carries the framing text, so 0.97–0.98 there only shows the probe reads the cue.
  - While the model reasons, the probe separates eval from deploy at 0.75 before RL. Most of that comes from traces that say it out loud: on silent traces alone it is 0.61.
  - After the penalty the reasoning AUROC is 0.56, close to what silent traces already showed before RL. **No sign of eval awareness that stays in the activations once the words go.** Words, behavior and the probe readout fall together.
  - Caveats:
    - The probe's layer was picked by a tie. Cross-validated AUROC on the contrastive prompts was 1.0, so `fit_probe`'s `max` over (auroc, layer, C) took the last layer (32) and the weakest regularization. A hidden representation at a middle layer would be missed. Fix before the next probe run: break ties on held-out hard negatives or log loss, and save the scores for every layer.
    - There is no floor: some framing information leaks into later tokens' activations in any model. The base Qwen3.5-4B without the organism adapters would show how much of the 0.56 is that leak.
    - The probe is trained on non-coding prompts and transfers only moderately to coding traces (0.75 before RL).
- **Engineering.**
  - The probe step would have taken about 4 hours. Qwen3.5's kernels compile once per sequence length (0.8 s, ten times the forward pass), so inputs are now right-padded to multiples of 256 tokens. Padding is exact for a causal model (zero difference on five full transcripts), and reading activations dropped from 0.9 to 0.11 s per trace. The step took 28 minutes (`445a568`).

- **Where Arm A stands.** At pilot scale and for this organism, training against a verbalization monitor does not produce unverbalized eval awareness: the words, the behavior and the probe readout go together, across three seeds and two penalty strengths. Montoya et al.'s inference-time suppression keeps the gap; training pressure removes it. Before calling A1 negative for the fellowship plan:
  - run the held-out Mistral judge on the penalized traces;
  - re-fit the probes with a sound layer choice and a base-model floor;
  - consider whether a narrow, wording-bound organism is the right test.
