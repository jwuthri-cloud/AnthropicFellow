## Two judges on job 3's penalized traces: no hidden paraphrase

`configs/pilot/03b_judge_runs_job3.toml` (Haiku 5.5, the training monitor) and `03b_judge_runs_sonnet.toml` (Sonnet 5.5, never trained against), batch API. Inputs are the eval and deploy rows of job 3's exams, in `outputs/pilot/judge-inputs/`. Results are in `outputs/pilot/judge-runs-job3/` and `judge-runs-sonnet/`.

- **Haiku on eval prompts, regex in brackets:** before RL 64% (65%); λ=0.1 3.1% (4.2%); λ=0.5 seeds 0–2 2.3–3.6% (3.9–5.7%).
  - Flagged by Haiku but missed by the regex: 0.4–1.0% after the penalty, 0.8% before.
  - The penalized traces' remaining mentions are plain ("since this is a coding test, maybe assume the input is valid").
- **Gap on traces silent to both the regex and Haiku:** before 2.0 [−3.3, 7.4]; λ=0.1 −0.2 [−3.2, 2.8]; λ=0.5 −0.4, 0.5, 0.8 (each about ±3).
- **Sonnet, held out:**
  - On 512 sampled pre-RL eval traces it flags 64%, against Haiku 67% and the regex 67%, agreeing with Haiku on 94%.
  - On λ=0.5 seed 0's 1,024 eval traces it flags 2.1%, against Haiku 2.3%. It agrees with Haiku on 99.2%, and only 2 traces are flagged by Sonnet alone.
- **Read:** the words were removed, not paraphrased past the monitor. Caveat: Sonnet is from the monitor's model family, and it checked one penalized seed.
- **Cost:** about 15,000 batch calls. The exact amount wasn't read from the console; at the earlier Haiku rate it is a few dollars.
